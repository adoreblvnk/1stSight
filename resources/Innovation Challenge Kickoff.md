How might we leverage automation, robotics, or data driven and AI driven solution to assist SCDF’s frontline responders, enhancing operational effectiveness and/or improving responder safety?

Develop a working prototype that demonstrates your proposed solution.

# Some of our Pain Points at a Glance

*   **Automation of Fire Report Generation**
    Manually crafting fire incident reports is labor-intensive
*   **Response Zone Time Boundary Mapping**
    Lack of a tool to dynamically map and calculate response time boundaries from fire stations based on real-time and historical traffic conditions
*   **Employing technology to optimize Security**
    Round-the-clock sentry deployment places a significant manpower burden on fire stations, contributing to fatigue to firefighters
*   **Optimize Individual IPPT Training result**
    IPPT training lacks personalization, making it difficult to track individual progress and tailor fitness routines to each officer
*   **Drug Stock and Expiry Management**
    Medication inventory and expiry tracking relies heavily on manual checks, leaving stations vulnerable to stockouts, overlooked expiries, and inefficient procurement

# Automation of Fire Report Generation

*   **Current situation:** Fire reports writing are not yet digitalised (manual) - notes of fire investigation are written manually, images of fire scene documentation are arranged manually on Word Document
*   **Impact:** Time-consuming for officers, risk of missing critical information
*   **Innovation idea:** AI-Powered Fire Report Generator that auto-populates standardised reports with images based on incident inputs, reducing errors and saving time

# Response Zone Time Boundary Mapping

*   **Current situation:** Response Zone Time Boundary mapping does not account for real-time or historical traffic conditions
*   **Impact:** Inconsistent response times, especially during peak hours
*   **Innovation idea:** A predictive mapping tool that calculates and visualises response time boundaries from each fire station using historical peak and non-peak traffic data, allowing commanders to optimise resource deployment

# Employing technology to optimize Security

*   **Current situation:** Sentry duties at fire stations require dedicated manpower around the clock
*   **Impact:** Manpower-intensive, limits deployment of personnel to core operational roles, fatigue
*   **Innovation idea:** Smart Sentry system using automated access control, vehicle plate recognition, facial recognition, and AI-powered surveillance to reduce reliance on manual guarding

*\* Not all stations are equipped with the Automated Access Control System*
*   Brainstorming for enhancements!

# Optimize Individual IPPT Training result

*   **Current situation:** Training programmes are generalised and do not account for individual fitness levels or weaknesses
*   **Impact:** Personnel may not improve effectively, risk of IPPT failures, no easy way for supervisors to monitor unit fitness trends
*   **Innovation idea:** AI-powered IPPT training dashboard that analyses individual performance data and recommends personalised exercise routines to target specific weaknesses

# Drug Stock and Expiry Management

*   **Current situation:** Medication inventory is tracked manually (in selected stations), with expiry checks done periodically
*   **Impact:** Risk of stockouts, wastage from expired medications, inefficient procurement
*   **Innovation idea:** AI-driven inventory system that monitors stock levels (in both store and emergency vehicles) in real time, forecasts demand, and alerts staff to medications approaching expiry before it becomes a problem

*\* Not all stations are equipped with the Automated Medical Store*
*   Brainstorming for enhancements!
