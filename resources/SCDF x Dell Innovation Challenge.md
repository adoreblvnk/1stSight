# Lifesavers' Innovation Challenge 2026

**Organized by:** SCDF (The Life Saving Force) & Dell Technologies

---

## The Challenge
**Core Question:** How might we leverage automation, robotics, or data-driven and AI-driven solutions to assist SCDF's frontline responders, enhancing operational effectiveness and/or improving responder safety?

**Requirement:** Develop a working prototype that demonstrates your proposed solution.
