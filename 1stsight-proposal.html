AI bodycam command dashboard

1stSight is a Command & Control dashboard, not a new bodycam: it turns bodycam footage into structured incident intelligence across incidents of any size.

What It Is

The main product is the C&C dashboard. For the prototype, firefighters only use a simple hands-free camera PWA to send sampled frames from a smartphone.

Dashboard Components

It reduces repeated radio updates, maintains a live timeline, links AI alerts to source-frame evidence, supports handover/reporting, and helps C&C prioritise hazards across one or multiple feeds.

Effective Use Of AI · 20%

How it works without adding frontline workload

Firefighters do not type, tag, or manage a complex app during the incident. The capture layer runs hands-free; AI structures visual cues for C&C, and officers confirm what becomes incident intelligence.

Hands-free feed

C&C workflow

Feasible and safe AI architecture

secure deployment + safe AI

Dell hosts the secure C&C dashboard. Gemini Flash/Pro can analyse sampled frames during the prototype, with Dell/NVIDIA options available for future deployment. AI assists; C&C decides.

Gemini Flash

Fast review for smoke, fire cues, exits, blocked access, and changing scene conditions.

Gemini Pro

Careful review for uncertain or high-impact moments, such as possible victim distress.

Dell deployment

Dell Cloud Native Platform and CSC Demo Studio can deploy, secure, and evaluate the prototype.

H

Human confirmation before AI suggestions become operational facts.

E

Evidence frames and timestamps stay attached to every alert.

S

Separate AI-suggested observations from confirmed facts.

R

Retain routine frames, evidence frames, and exports under different rules.

N

No facial recognition or identity matching without explicit governance.

A

Audit log records alerts, confirmations, dismissals, degraded feeds, and exports.

D

Degraded connectivity is shown clearly, not hidden.

Decision contract

1stSight does not command firefighters. It organises what C&C already needs to see: confidence, source frame, timestamp, and audit trail.
